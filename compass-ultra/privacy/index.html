Privacy policy
Compass Ultra for Android

Your position stays on your phone.

This policy explains what Compass Ultra does with information about you. It's short because the app does very little with it.

Effective November 19, 2026

On your device

Location, waypoints, sensors and the camera are used on your phone and never sent to us.

No account, no analytics

No sign-up, no tracking of how you use the app, no crash reporting.

One ad, from Google

The free version shows one banner from Google AdMob. Pro removes it.

Purchases by Google Play

Google handles payment. We never see your card or payment details.

Information that stays on your device

Compass Ultra uses the following only on your phone. Crafty Soft never receives any of it.

Location

Precise and approximate location, for true north (magnetic declination), sun and moon positions, altitude, speed, and the distance and direction to your waypoints. The last known position is saved so these work before a new GPS fix.

Waypoints

The names and coordinates of places you mark or enter.

Sensors

Motion and magnetic sensors and the barometer, for heading, tilt, the level and barometric altitude. Readings are processed live and not stored.

Camera

The AR view shows the camera image with the compass drawn over it. Images are never saved or sent anywhere.

Settings

Your choices (theme, units, faces, widget options) and whether Pro is unlocked, so the app works offline.

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's settings and waypoints in your device backup to your own Google account. That backup is managed by Android and Google, not by Crafty Soft, and you can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

When you use Open in maps, the waypoint's coordinates and name are passed to the maps app you choose. When you copy coordinates, they go to your phone's clipboard.

Advertising in the free version

The free version shows one banner ad from Google AdMob, at the bottom of the pulled-up readings sheet. The Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and send to Google information such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), ad interactions and diagnostic data, in order to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

Compass Ultra does not give the ad SDK your GPS position or your waypoints.

Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and Ad privacy choices in Settings lets you change your answer. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's settings. Google's use of this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Compass Ultra Pro removes the ad, and the ad SDK is not started when the app opens for Pro users.

Purchases

Compass Ultra Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and Crafty Soft does not receive your payment details. The app asks Google Play only whether Pro has been bought on your Google account, and stores that yes-or-no answer on your phone.

Notifications

If you set a home-screen widget to Always live, Android shows an ongoing notification while it runs. The app asks for notification permission for that purpose only.

What we don't do

  • Create accounts or ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • Sell, rent or share your information.
  • Send your location or waypoints to any server.

Deleting your information

Because the app's data lives only on your phone, you can delete it at any time: delete waypoints in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Children

Compass Ultra is not directed at children under 13, and Crafty Soft does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy
